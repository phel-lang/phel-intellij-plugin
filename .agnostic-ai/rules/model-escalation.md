# Model selection

Use the standard model by default. Escalate to the frontier model for architectural or ambiguous grammar work, large cross-package scope, unclear requirements, hard debugging (PSI and parser edge cases, flaky tests, completion ordering), a failed attempt, or confidence under 0.8 on non-trivial work. Escalate early rather than loop on failures. Stay standard for routine work.
