Reports the source forms Phel 0.50 superseded, mirroring the compiler's own --warn-deprecations output in the editor.

The rule behind the interop entries is that php/ means host access, and is never a second spelling for something Phel already says the Clojure way. The superseded forms keep working for every 1.x release and remain the target the Clojure-style shorthand expands to, so they still appear in completion; they are the candidates for removal at the next major.

SupersededWrite instead
(php/new Foo arg)(Foo. arg) or (new Foo arg)
(php/-> obj (method arg))(.method obj arg) or (.-field obj)
(php/:: Foo (method arg))(Foo/method arg) or Foo/CONST
(set-var v x)(alter-var-root #'v f), or (set! v x) for the current binding frame

The rest of the php/ family is not reported. php/aget, php/aset, php/apush, php/aunset, php/oset, php/ref and php/callable each reach a PHP capability Phel has no other word for, so they are current rather than superseded.

No quick-fix is offered: every replacement here reorders or re-nests the call's arguments, so there is no text substitution that would be correct in general.
